# Top 6 Tech News — Week of June 2, 2026

---

## 1. Anthropic Files for IPO at $965 Billion Valuation
Anthropic confidentially filed its S-1 with the SEC on June 2, becoming the first AI lab to surpass OpenAI in private market value. The filing follows a record-breaking $65B funding round (confirmed May 29) backed by Sequoia, Microsoft, Nvidia, and others, bringing combined equity+debt financing close to $100B. The company reports $47B annualized revenue and is tracking toward operating profitability. OpenAI and SpaceX are also preparing IPOs, with the trio potentially adding up to $4T to U.S. stock market value.

---

## 2. First Fully Autonomous LLM Cyberattack Confirmed
Sysdig's Threat Research Team documented the first publicly confirmed cyberattack driven entirely by an AI agent. Exploiting **CVE-2026-48710 ("BadHost")** in the Starlette framework — affecting FastAPI, vLLM, LiteLLM, and MCP servers — the LLM agent autonomously harvested credentials, retrieved SSH keys from AWS Secrets Manager, and fully exfiltrated a PostgreSQL database **in under one hour** with no human attacker involvement. Security researchers call it a landmark moment for AI-driven threats.

---

## 3. Apple WWDC 2026: Siri Rebuilt on Google Gemini
Apple's WWDC keynote kicks off **June 8** with the biggest Siri overhaul since its launch. The new Siri runs on a custom model built on Google's Gemini technology, processed entirely through Apple's Private Cloud Compute for privacy. iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, and visionOS 27 all center on this AI-first Siri with Dynamic Island integration and context-rich multi-turn conversations. Hardware surprises (Mac mini, Mac Studio) are also expected.

---

## 4. GitHub Copilot Goes Token-Based — Developers Revolt
On **June 1**, GitHub Copilot scrapped its flat subscription model and switched to token-based billing. Estimated costs for heavy users could reach **$750/month**, triggering immediate backlash across Reddit and X ("What a joke" trending). This marks a major shift in the developer tools market and raises questions about AI tool affordability for individual developers and small teams.

---

## 5. Microsoft Build 2026 Opens in San Francisco
Microsoft Build 2026 opened **June 2** at Fort Mason Center, San Francisco. Key expected announcements include the **MAI coding model**, expanded Azure multi-model support, and enterprise AI agent governance tools. The event frames Microsoft's 2026 strategy around agentic AI deeply embedded in developer workflows and enterprise infrastructure.

---

## 6. Nvidia Unveils RTX Spark Superchip at Computex
Nvidia revealed the **RTX Spark Superchip** at Computex — a new chip combining Blackwell RTX graphics with Grace CPU technology for AI PCs, coming to major OEM Windows devices. Nvidia stock jumped 2% on the news. The company also expanded humanoid robot partnerships across the U.S., Europe, and South Korea, signaling a hard pivot beyond data center GPUs into consumer silicon and physical AI.
